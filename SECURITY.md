# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security problem in Lunite, please report it privately by email to **luniteapp@gmail.com**, with "Security" in the subject line. Please don't open a public GitHub issue for it.

Useful things to include:

- the Lunite version (the top of the window and ABOUT show it) and your Windows version
- what the problem is and what someone could do with it
- the steps to reproduce it, or a small proof of concept
- whether you think anyone is already using it

Please leave out personal information you don't need to share, like your PC's name, your user folder or your network addresses.

## Testing your own copy

Looking into your own copy of Lunite on your own PC, to find a security problem and report it privately, is welcome. Treat this as the written permission section 2 of the [LICENSE](LICENSE) asks for, for that purpose only. It doesn't cover sharing a modified copy or testing on someone else's PC without their say.

## What happens next

- Lunite is made by one person, so a reply can take a while.
- I'll confirm the problem, fix it, and ship the fix through the signed update channel. The release notes will say a security problem was fixed.
- If you'd like credit in the release notes, say so and tell me the name to use.

## Bounty

There is no bug bounty. Lunite can't pay for reports, but every report is read and taken seriously.

## Scope

In scope: `Lunite.exe` from this repository's Releases page, its built-in check-up and fix engines, and its update check.

Out of scope: copies of Lunite downloaded from anywhere else, and other companies' programs that Lunite reads or removes.
